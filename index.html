Follow the three-table method

0 · Count the conductors

Add up the quantity of every conductor listed for the raceway.

1 · Table 6 series: conductor area

Match the insulation designation, voltage, jacket, construction and size. Read the area for that quantity of conductors, then add the group areas when sizes or insulation differ.

2 · Table 8: allowable percentage

For ordinary non-lead-sheathed insulated conductors: one = 53%; two = 31%; three or more = 40%.

3 · Table 9 series: raceway capacity

Use 9C for 53%, 9E for 31% or 9G for 40%. Choose the correct raceway column, then the first trade size with enough allowable area.

A Table 9 allowable-fill area already includes the percentage. Compare the conductor area directly with that value. Do not multiply it by 40% a second time.
